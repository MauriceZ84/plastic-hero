The problem we can't ignore

Plastic ends up on our beaches, in our rivers and in the animals that live there. And every year, more is added than the year before.

From plastic waste to clean oil

No burning, no landfill. Plastic goes back to where it came from.

Plastic is collected

Plastic waste that would otherwise end up in nature or a landfill is collected, sorted and prepared for processing.

Heated, never burned

In large pyrolysis reactors the plastic is heated until it breaks down. The flame never touches it, and the gases released power the process itself.

A new raw material

Every 1,000 kg of plastic becomes up to 900 litres of oil, used again to make new plastic. Less waste in nature, less new oil from the ground.

And this is where you come in

Every person leaves around 50 kg of plastic behind each year. With Corsair you can have that same amount removed from the environment, for yourself and for your family.

Your contribution is backed by CSR Plastic Credits on the blockchain, so your impact is visible and can be checked by anyone. That's how ordinary people become Plastic Heroes.

The Technology

Pyrolysis literally means "separating with heat". The plastic is never burned: the flame never touches it. Corsair likes to call its reactors the biggest pizza ovens in the world.

From waste to oil in four steps

  1. Plastic waste is sorted and made as clean and dry as possible.
  2. A rotating reactor heats a batch of 8,000 to 9,000 kg of plastic.
  3. The gases released are fed back into the oven, so after about eight hours the process runs on its own energy.
  4. The vapour condenses into oil: a heavy fraction similar to diesel and a light fraction similar to petrol.

CSR Plastic Credit

Every time Corsair removes a kilo of plastic from the environment, 10 CSR Plastic Credits come into circulation. A credit is a digital receipt proving that real plastic was removed.

The credits live on the Ethereum blockchain, so every credit and every transfer is public and can be checked by anyone.

How to become plastic neutral

  1. Work out your plastic footprint. On average that's around 50 kg per person per year.
  2. Have the same amount of plastic removed through a plastic-neutral package.
  3. Receive the matching credits and your "I am Plastic Neutral" certificate.

Certification & Partners
